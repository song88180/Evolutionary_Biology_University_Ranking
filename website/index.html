The methodology

One paper.
One clear rule.

Only original research with evolution as a major scientific question or conclusion is counted. Articles must have first appeared online in one of the 11 selected journals during the publication window.

Download all paper contributions ↘
01

Weight the paper

Each qualifying paper starts with its journal's 2025 impact factor. No citation counts, reputation measures, or university-size adjustment are used.

02

Split the credit

Divide that weight equally among all distinct corresponding-author institutions. Multiple corresponding authors at the same institution count once. Non-corresponding affiliations receive nothing.

03

Rank the universities

Add each institution's shares, then rank only universities in the ARWU 2026 top 1,000. Institutions outside that pool keep their shares; their points are never reassigned.

The source set

Journal weights

The same 2025 journal impact factor is used for every qualifying paper in a journal.

This site presents the project owner's final ranking of the reviewed dataset. Correspondence for 347 of 2,378 papers uses explicitly marked OpenAlex author information; the remainder uses publisher or repository evidence. All paper-level credits are available above for independent inspection.
